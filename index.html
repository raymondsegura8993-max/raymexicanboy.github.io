How to enter a giveaway

Follow these steps whenever a giveaway is active:

  1. Join the RayMexicanBoy Discord server.
  2. Open the Active Giveaways section on this website and press Enter Giveaway.
  3. Then open the giveaways channel in Discord and complete that giveaway's rules.
  4. Use the username saved on your RayGiveaway profile if the giveaway asks for it.
