Enter a RayGiveaway

Entering is simple. Everything happens on this website:

  1. Create a free RayGiveaway account or sign in.
  2. Open Active Giveaways and choose a prize.
  3. Press Enter giveaway. You will see a confirmation when your entry is saved.
  4. One entry is allowed per account for each giveaway.
  5. When the timer ends, the admin selects a winner and the result is posted here.

You do not need Discord to enter. Never share your password or payment information to enter a giveaway.
